# Dubbing Studio

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary user is the operator reviewing his own private multilingual recordings on a
trusted local computer or through his private Tailscale network. The job is to turn uncertain
machine output into explicit, correction-lineaged evidence without handling repository files
or command-line internals during review.

## Product Purpose

Dubbing Studio processes long English, Russian, Romanian and Korean recordings locally,
surfaces uncertainty honestly, and makes the smallest necessary human review easy. Success
means the operator can listen, approve or correct, and export a validated transcript while
failed or unresolved material remains blocked from GIGA admission.

## Positioning

A private, provider-neutral transcription control plane where evidence earns admission rather
than merely producing plausible text.

## Brand Personality

Calm, exact and trustworthy. Copy is direct and operational; the interface should reduce the
operator's cognitive load without disguising uncertainty or technical state.

## Anti-references

Avoid analytics-dashboard theatre, decorative AI-workflow styling, confidence values that were
not emitted by the provider, modal-heavy review flows, and any interface that makes approval
easier to trigger accidentally than correction.

## Design Principles

- Put the current evidence decision at the centre of the screen.
- Preserve original text, timestamps, language and correction lineage.
- Make the safe path the fast path through keyboard control and automatic progression.
- Distinguish approval, correction and unresolved speech in words as well as colour.
- Keep private media local and never imply that review automatically admits evidence to GIGA.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls, visible focus, keyboard-complete operation, WCAG AA contrast, generous
touch targets, reduced-motion support and layouts that remain usable on a phone. Multilingual
scripts must render without transliteration or substitution.
